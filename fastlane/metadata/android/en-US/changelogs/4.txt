Keyholm's first release on fdroid! It's quite feature complete already when it comes to single-device passkeys:

- Passkey creation/use as an Android passkey service
- Passkey management
- Extensive settings for configuring exactly how to create/use your passkeys
- PRF support
- Passkey list export/import
- Icon pack support
- Editable RP name, username and display name
- Non-discoverable passkeys

This should be the same as v0.1, which will be the first release with backwards compatibility.
